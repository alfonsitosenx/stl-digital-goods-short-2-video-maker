# Stl/Digital goods short 2 video maker

Windows app by [Excess Pro Net LLC](https://excesspronet.com) that turns `.stl` models and digital-product images into short marketing videos and keyword-rich listing copy — ready for Etsy, Pinterest, Reels and TikTok.

**This repository hosts the downloads only — it contains no source code.**

## Download

**[Latest release](https://github.com/alfonsitosenx/stl-digital-goods-short-2-video-maker/releases/latest)** — `STLDigitalGoodsShort2VideoMaker-Setup-2.0.3.exe` (94 MB)

The installer bundles the complete 3D rendering and video engines, so nothing else needs to be installed.

## What it does

### Spinning video studio
- **Turntable MP4s from any STL** — rendered offline with VTK, no cloud upload
- **11 spin modes** — shop turntable, orbit left/right, pitch, barrel, 15°/30°/45° tilt, wobble, spiral, swing, up/down
- **20+ starting-view presets** — natural, back, side, top-down, bottom-up, overhead, iso and hero angles
- **4 studio backgrounds** and a custom model colour
- 5, 7, 10 or 15 seconds at 720p, 1080p or square (Etsy-recommended)

### AI marketing studio
- **Listing copy from your product images** — title, multi-paragraph description, 13 tags, caption and per-slide text
- **Vision AI via OpenRouter or Alibaba Cloud** — bring your own API key, pick any vision model
- **Auto-Fill needs no AI key at all** — builds copy from folder contents and file sizes
- **30 video styles** across Classic, Modern Dynamic, Cinematic Focus and Social Media Pro
- **4 aspect ratios** — 1:1 square (Etsy), 9:16 (Reels/TikTok), 2:3 (Pinterest), 4:5 (Facebook/Instagram)
- Optional watermark, background music and AI voiceover
- **Push a draft listing straight to Etsy** with images and video attached

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- 8 GB RAM recommended
- Graphics with OpenGL 3.2 support (for the 3D turntable)
- Internet only for licence activation and the optional AI copy features — rendering is fully offline
